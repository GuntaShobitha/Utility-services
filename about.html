auto_stories Our story

Started with one truck. Now serving 35 cities.

Founded in 2007 by a small team of environmental engineers, STACKLY began with a single waste route in an industrial suburb. Today we operate across three continents, running everything from smart bin networks to municipal-scale biogas plants.

What has never changed is our belief that utility work should be measured by outcomes — landfill diverted, water restored, emissions avoided — not just tons hauled.
